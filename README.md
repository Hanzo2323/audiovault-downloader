## ✨ What's New in Android v1.2.0
- **🔄 In-App Auto-Update:** Automatically checks for new Android updates on launch (or manually via **Settings & Storage -> Check for Updates**), downloads the latest APK with a live progress bar, and opens the Android package installer directly.
- **⭐ Favorites Section:** Tap the Star button on any movie or TV show card to save it to your persistent Favorites list, accessible anytime via the **Favorites** filter chip.
- **🌙 Smart Sleep Timer:** Automatically pause playback after 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes, or right at the **End of Current Track** with a live countdown inside the player.
- **⏱️ Jump to Time:** Seek directly to any exact timestamp in long movies or episodes by entering Hours, Minutes, and Seconds.
- **🎧 Bluetooth Headset Seek Controls:** Next/Previous media buttons on Bluetooth headphones and headsets jump +30s forward and -30s backward.

## 🛠️ Core Android Features & TalkBack Optimizations
- **📦 Instant TV Episode Streaming without Full ZIP Download:** Browse and stream individual episodes directly from AudioVault season ZIP archives without downloading the full season archive first.
- **📂 Full Offline Playback:** Play downloaded MP3 movies and browse/play episodes inside downloaded season ZIP archives completely offline.
- **🗣️ 100% TalkBack Friendly:** Uses movie audio attributes so TalkBack speech smoothly lowers playback volume instead of pausing playback, with clearly labeled controls for -30s, -5s, Play/Pause, +5s, +30s, Speed, Pitch, and Dynamic Audio Normalization.
- **💾 Automatic Resume Playback & Cache Manager:** Remembers your exact playback position for every movie and episode, with one-tap temporary stream cache cleanup in **Settings & Storage**.
